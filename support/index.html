Support

Found a bug, a wrong letter name, or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub.

How do the weeks work?

Gamli brings 1 or 2 new letters a week, whichever a grown-up picked at setup. The letters don't come in aleph-bet order: easy, common ones come first and look-alikes like ב and כ are kept apart. Between new letters, the games practice the ones already met.

Can we go faster, or slower?

Yes. Open Grown-up settings (the gear on the journey screen, after a quick sum) to change the pace or unlock the next letter right away.

I don't hear Gamli

Turn up the volume. Gamli uses the device's built-in voices; if the Hebrew letter names are silent, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Hebrew and download a voice.

Which pronunciation should I pick?

Modern Israeli is how Hebrew is spoken in Israel today. Ashkenazi is traditional in many synagogues and Hebrew schools in North America and Europe (for example, "Shabbos"). Sephardi / Mizrahi keeps a deep throaty ח and ע. You can change it in Grown-up settings.

Starting over

Grown-up settings → Start over clears the setup and all progress on the device.
