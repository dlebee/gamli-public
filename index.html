The journey

Five journeys. Five graduations.

צַעַד אַחַר צַעַדStep by step.

Gamli walks your child from their very first letter to reading whole sentences out loud, one small step at a time.

  1. 1

    Letters אוֹתִיּוֹתLetters

    One or two new letters a week, in an order that keeps look-alikes apart. Find them, color them, and catch them as they sail by.

    א ב גaleph, bet, gimel
  2. 2

Questions

Grown-up questions, answered.

יֵשׁ שְׁאֵלוֹת?Any questions?

Who is Gamli for?

Young children just starting the aleph-bet, and the grown-ups who play along with them. Grown-ups learning to read Hebrew enjoy it too.

I don't read Hebrew. Can I still help?

Yes. Every Hebrew line in the app has English right under it, and Gamli says the letters and words out loud, so you can learn right alongside your child.

אוֹת אַחַר אוֹתOne letter at a time.

Why aren't the letters in aleph-bet order?

Easy, common letters come first, and look-alikes like ב and כ are kept apart so they don't get mixed up. Between new letters, the games practice the ones already met.

Which pronunciation should I pick?

Modern Israeli is how Hebrew is spoken in Israel today. Ashkenazi is traditional in many synagogues and Hebrew schools in North America and Europe (for example, "Shabbos"). Sephardi / Mizrahi keeps a deep, throaty ח and ע. You can change it any time in Grown-up settings.

Can we go faster, or slower?

Yes. Open Grown-up settings (the gear on the journey screen, after a quick sum) to change the pace or unlock the next letter right away.

How much does it cost?

Gamli is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

What data does Gamli collect?

None. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your child's name and progress stay in a small file on the device, and we never see it. The privacy policy has the details.

Which devices does it run on?

Gamli is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. An Android version is on the way.
