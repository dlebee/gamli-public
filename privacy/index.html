Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

The short version: Gamli doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app knows stays on your device.

Who we are

Gamli is an app that teaches children the Hebrew aleph-bet, built by David Lebee. This policy covers the Gamli apps and this website.

What the app stores

During setup, a grown-up enters the child's first name and, optionally, their name in Hebrew letters, so Gamli can greet them and teach those letters first. The app also saves the chosen pronunciation, the number of new letters each week, and which letters have been found or missed in its games.

All of this stays in a small file on the device. We don't have a server, so we never see it. Deleting the app deletes it, and a grown-up can erase it any time with Grown-up settings → Start over. If the device backs up apps to iCloud, the file may be included in that backup, which Apple's privacy terms cover.

What the app doesn't collect

We don't collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers, usage analytics or crash reports. The app has no sign-in, no in-app purchases, no advertising and no third-party code that tracks anyone.

Audio

Gamli's English lines are recordings that ship inside the app, and Hebrew is spoken by the device's built-in text-to-speech, which runs on the device. The app doesn't record audio and doesn't use the microphone or camera.

Grown-up settings

Settings are behind a parent gate, a short sum a child is unlikely to solve, so children can't change them by accident.

Children

Gamli is made for children. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13, and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting any.

Beta testing

If you test a pre-release version through Apple TestFlight, Apple may share basic information with us, such as your name or email, the device you used, crash reports, and any feedback you choose to send. We use this only to fix and improve the app. Apple's privacy policy covers how Apple handles that data.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, GitHub and Google may log your IP address and browser details when you visit. We don't add any analytics or cookies of our own.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub, or see the support page.
